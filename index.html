核心心理学词汇

Core Psychology Terms — The building blocks for understanding the mind.

心理学 ⭐ START xīnlǐ xué psychology
中文释义研究人类心理现象、精神功能和行为的科学。
English MeaningThe scientific study of the human mind, mental functions, and behavior.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 心 + 理 + 学 = 心理学
心 (xīn) = heart; mind  |  理 (lǐ) = principle; logic; to manage  |  学 (xué) = study; science
→ The study of the mind's principles.
📝 Example 她对心理学非常感兴趣。 She is very interested in psychology.
心理状态 xīnlǐ zhuàngtài mental state
中文释义一个人在某段时间内心理活动的情况和特征。
English MeaningThe condition and characteristics of a person's mental activity during a period of time.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 心理 + 状态 = 心理状态
心理 (xīnlǐ) = psychology; mind  |  状态 (zhuàngtài) = state; condition
→ Mental state or condition.
📝 Example 他的心理状态最近不太好。 His mental state hasn't been great lately.
意识 yìshí consciousness; awareness
中文释义人对外界和自身的觉察与认识。
English MeaningA person's perception and recognition of the outside world and oneself.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 意 + 识 = 意识
意 (yì) = meaning; intention; awareness  |  识 (shí) = to know; to recognize
→ Awareness and recognition.
📝 Example 他失去了意识。 He lost consciousness.
无意识 wúyìshí unconscious; subconscious
中文释义不被自己察觉的心理活动。
English MeaningMental activity that one is not aware of.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 无 + 意识 = 无意识
无 (wú) = without; no  |  意识 (yìshí) = consciousness
→ Without consciousness.
📝 Example 这个动作是无意识的。 This action was unconscious.
认知 rènzhī cognition
中文释义人认识外界事物的过程，包括思考、学习、记忆等。
English MeaningThe process of knowing the world, including thinking, learning, and memory.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 认 + 知 = 认知
认 (rèn) = to recognize; to know  |  知 (zhī) = to know; knowledge
→ Recognizing and knowing.
📝 Example 孩子的认知能力在不断发展。 A child's cognitive ability is constantly developing.
记忆 jìyì memory
中文释义大脑储存和回想过去经验的能力。
English MeaningThe brain's ability to store and recall past experiences.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 记 + 忆 = 记忆
记 (jì) = to remember; to record  |  忆 (yì) = to recall; to recollect
→ Recording and recalling.
📝 Example 童年的记忆很美好。 Childhood memories are very beautiful.
感知 gǎnzhī perception
中文释义通过感官认识外界事物的过程。
English MeaningThe process of understanding the world through the senses.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 感 + 知 = 感知
感 (gǎn) = to feel; sensation  |  知 (zhī) = to know
→ Feeling and knowing.
📝 Example 婴儿通过感官来感知世界。 Babies perceive the world through their senses.

情绪与情感

Emotions & Feelings — The inner weather of the heart and mind. Positive, negative, and everything in between.

情绪 ⭐ FOCUS qíngxù mood; emotion
中文释义人对事物的态度体验，如喜、怒、哀、乐等。
English MeaningA person's experiential attitude toward things, such as joy, anger, sadness, and happiness.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 情 + 绪 = 情绪
情 (qíng) = emotion; feeling  |  绪 (xù) = thread; state of mind; mood
→ The threads of feeling; mood state.
📝 Example 你要学会控制自己的情绪。 You need to learn to control your emotions.
坎坷 ⭐ FEATURED kǎnkě full of frustrations; bumpy; rough (of life/path)
中文释义道路坑坑洼洼不平坦。比喻人生不顺利，充满困难挫折。
English MeaningA road full of bumps and holes. Metaphor for a life that is not smooth; full of hardships and setbacks.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 坎 + 坷 = 坎坷
坎 (kǎn) = pit; hole; threshold; difficulty  |  坷 (kě) = uneven; bumpy
→ Full of pits and bumps — a rough, difficult journey.
📝 Example 那位阿姨怎么这么坎坷，一辈子吃了这么多苦。 That auntie has had such a rough life, enduring so much hardship.
开心 kāixīn happy; joyful
中文释义心情愉快，感到高兴。
English MeaningFeeling cheerful and delighted.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 开 + 心 = 开心
开 (kāi) = open; to bloom  |  心 (xīn) = heart
→ Heart blooming open — happy.
📝 Example 今天玩得真开心！ I had so much fun today! (Lit. so happy.)
快乐 kuàilè happy; cheerful; joyful
中文释义感到幸福和满足的心情。
English MeaningA feeling of happiness and contentment.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 快 + 乐 = 快乐
快 (kuài) = fast; pleased; joyful  |  乐 (lè) = happy; music; pleasure
→ Pleasantly joyful.
📝 Example 祝你生日快乐！ Happy birthday to you!
兴奋 xīngfèn excited; thrilled
中文释义因为期待或刺激而充满活力和激动的状态。
English MeaningA state of energy and agitation due to anticipation or stimulation.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 兴 + 奋 = 兴奋
兴 (xīng) = to rise; to flourish; excitement  |  奋 (fèn) = to exert; to be roused
→ Roused and rising with excitement.
📝 Example 听到这个消息，他很兴奋。 He was very excited to hear the news.
满足 mǎnzú content; satisfied
中文释义感到愿望实现了，已经足够，不再需要更多。
English MeaningFeeling that one's wishes have been fulfilled; enough, not needing more.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 满 + 足 = 满足
满 (mǎn) = full; satisfied  |  足 (zú) = enough; sufficient
→ Full and enough — content.
📝 Example 他对现在的生活很满足。 He is very content with his current life.
幸福 xìngfú happy; blissful; blessed
中文释义生活美满，心里感到温暖和满足的持久状态。
English MeaningA lasting state of warmth and satisfaction in life.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 幸 + 福 = 幸福
幸 (xìng) = fortunate; lucky  |  福 (fú) = blessing; happiness; good fortune
→ Fortunate and blessed — deep happiness.
📝 Example 一家人在一起就是幸福。 Being together as a family is happiness.
焦虑 jiāolǜ anxiety; anxious
中文释义对未来的事情感到担心、紧张和不安。
English MeaningFeeling worried, nervous, and uneasy about future events.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 焦 + 虑 = 焦虑
焦 (jiāo) = burnt; scorched; anxious  |  虑 (lǜ) = to worry; to consider
→ Burnt with worry — anxious and concerned.
📝 Example 考试前他感到非常焦虑。 He felt very anxious before the exam.
紧张 jǐnzhāng nervous; tense
中文释义精神处于高度戒备状态，感到不安和压力。
English MeaningMentally on high alert; feeling uneasy and pressured.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 紧 + 张 = 紧张
紧 (jǐn) = tight; tense  |  张 (zhāng) = to stretch; to spread
→ Tightly stretched — nervous.
📝 Example 第一次上台，他非常紧张。 On stage for the first time, he was very nervous.
悲伤 bēishāng sad; sorrowful; grieved
中文释义因为失去或不幸而感到心痛和难过。
English MeaningFeeling heartache and distress due to loss or misfortune.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 悲 + 伤 = 悲伤
悲 (bēi) = sad; sorrowful  |  伤 (shāng) = wound; injury; to hurt
→ Sorrow that wounds — grief.
📝 Example 听到这个消息，她非常悲伤。 She was very sad when she heard the news.
难过 nánguò sad; grieved; heartbroken
中文释义心里不舒服，感到伤心。
English MeaningFeeling uncomfortable in the heart; sad.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 难 + 过 = 难过
难 (nán) = difficult; hard to bear  |  过 (guò) = to pass; to go through
→ Hard to get through — heartbroken.
📝 Example 别难过了，一切都会好起来的。 Don't be sad; everything will be better.
失望 shīwàng disappointed; let down
中文释义希望落了空，感到没有达到预期而失落。
English MeaningHope has fallen through; feeling let down because expectations weren't met.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 失 + 望 = 失望
失 (shī) = to lose; to fail  |  望 (wàng) = hope; expectation
→ Losing hope — disappointed.
📝 Example 他对结果感到非常失望。 He was very disappointed with the result.
愤怒 fènnù angry; furious; indignant
中文释义因为受到不公平待遇或伤害而产生强烈的生气情绪。
English MeaningA strong feeling of anger due to unfair treatment or harm.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 愤 + 怒 = 愤怒
愤 (fèn) = indignation; resentment  |  怒 (nù) = anger; rage
→ Indignant anger — furious.
📝 Example 他的行为让大家感到愤怒。 His behavior made everyone angry.
委屈 wěiqu feel wronged; aggrieved
中文释义受到不公平的指责或对待，心里感到难受和冤枉。
English MeaningFeeling hurt and unjustly treated due to unfair blame or treatment.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 委 + 屈 = 委屈
委 (wěi) = to entrust; to bend; injustice  |  屈 (qū) = to bend; to submit; wronged
→ Bent by injustice — aggrieved.
📝 Example 被冤枉的时候，她觉得很委屈。 When wrongly accused, she felt very wronged.
羞愧 xiūkuì ashamed; mortified
中文释义因为自己的错误或不足而感到丢脸和惭愧。
English MeaningFeeling embarrassed and ashamed due to one's own mistakes or shortcomings.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 羞 + 愧 = 羞愧
羞 (xiū) = shy; ashamed; embarrassed  |  愧 (kuì) = ashamed; remorseful
→ Doubly ashamed — mortified.
📝 Example 他对自己的行为感到羞愧。 He felt ashamed of his actions.
内疚 nèijiù guilty; remorseful
中文释义因为做了错事或伤害了别人而感到心里不安和自责。
English MeaningFeeling uneasy and self-reproachful for having done wrong or hurt someone.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 内 + 疚 = 内疚
内 (nèi) = inside; inner  |  疚 (jiù) = remorse; guilt
→ Inner guilt — remorse.
📝 Example 他因为撒谎而感到内疚。 He felt guilty for lying.
后悔 hòuhuǐ regret; feel remorse
中文释义对已经发生的事情感到不应该那样做，希望可以重新来过。
English MeaningFeeling that something in the past should not have been done; wishing to do it over.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 后 + 悔 = 后悔
后 (hòu) = after; later  |  悔 (huǐ) = to regret; to repent
→ Afterwards regret.
📝 Example 我后悔没有早点告诉她真相。 I regret not telling her the truth earlier.
嫉妒 jídù jealous; envious
中文释义因为别人拥有自己想要的东西而感到不满和怨恨。
English MeaningFeeling discontent and resentment because others have what one wants.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 嫉 + 妒 = 嫉妒
嫉 (jí) = to envy; to hate  |  妒 (dù) = jealousy; envy
→ Envious jealousy.
📝 Example 不要嫉妒别人的成功。 Don't be jealous of others' success.
感动 gǎndòng touched; moved (emotionally)
中文释义因为别人的好意或感人事情而心里受到触动。
English MeaningThe heart is stirred by others' kindness or touching events.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 感 + 动 = 感动
感 (gǎn) = to feel; emotion  |  动 (dòng) = to move; movement
→ Emotionally moved.
📝 Example 他的故事让我非常感动。 His story moved me deeply.
感激 gǎnjī grateful; thankful
中文释义因为别人的帮助而心里充满感谢。
English MeaningFilled with thanks because of others' help.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 感 + 激 = 感激
感 (gǎn) = to feel  |  激 (jī) = to arouse; to excite; intense
→ Intensely felt thanks — grateful.
📝 Example 我非常感激你的帮助。 I am very grateful for your help.
孤独 gūdú lonely; solitary
中文释义一个人感到孤单，没有陪伴和理解。
English MeaningFeeling alone without companionship or understanding.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 孤 + 独 = 孤独
孤 (gū) = alone; orphaned; isolated  |  独 (dú) = alone; single; independent
→ Alone and solitary — lonely.
📝 Example 有时候，他会感到深深的孤独。 Sometimes, he feels deeply lonely.
困惑 kùnhuò confused; perplexed
中文释义感到不明白，不知道该怎么办。
English MeaningFeeling not understanding; not knowing what to do.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 困 + 惑 = 困惑
困 (kùn) = trapped; difficult; sleepy  |  惑 (huò) = puzzled; confused; doubt
→ Trapped in confusion — perplexed.
📝 Example 这个问题让我很困惑。 This question makes me very confused.
纠结 jiūjié entangled; conflicted; struggling to decide
中文释义心里矛盾，难以做出选择或放下某事。
English MeaningInternally conflicted; hard to make a choice or let go of something.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 纠 + 结 = 纠结
纠 (jiū) = to entangle; to gather  |  结 (jié) = knot; to tie; to form
→ Tangled in knots — conflicted.
📝 Example 我在两个选择之间纠结了很久。 I struggled between the two choices for a long time.
压抑 yāyì repressed; oppressed; stifled
中文释义情绪、想法被压制在心里，无法自由表达。
English MeaningEmotions or thoughts are suppressed inside, unable to be freely expressed.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 压 + 抑 = 压抑
压 (yā) = to press; to suppress  |  抑 (yì) = to restrain; to repress
→ Pressed down and restrained — repressed.
📝 Example 不要把情绪压抑在心里。 Don't bottle up your emotions.
安心 ānxīn feel at ease; relieved; peace of mind
中文释义心情平静，没有担忧和不安。
English MeaningMind is calm; free from worry and unease.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 安 + 心 = 安心
安 (ān) = peaceful; safe; calm  |  心 (xīn) = heart; mind
→ A peaceful heart — at ease.
📝 Example 知道她安全到家，我才安心。 Only after knowing she got home safely could I feel at ease.
自豪 zìháo proud; pride
中文释义因为自己或与自己相关的人取得了成就而感到光荣。
English MeaningFeeling honored because of one's own or a related person's achievements.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 自 + 豪 = 自豪
自 (zì) = self  |  豪 (háo) = grand; heroic; proud
→ Self-grandeur — proud.
📝 Example 父母为孩子的成功感到自豪。 Parents are proud of their child's success.
自卑感 zìbēi gǎn inferiority complex; feeling of inferiority
中文释义觉得自己不如别人，看不起自己的感觉。
English MeaningA feeling of being less than others; looking down on oneself.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 自 + 卑 + 感 = 自卑感
自 (zì) = self  |  卑 (bēi) = low; humble; inferior  |  感 (gǎn) = feeling
→ A feeling of self-lowliness.
📝 Example 他因为个子矮而有自卑感。 He has an inferiority complex because of his short height.
自尊心 zìzūnxīn self-esteem; self-respect
中文释义尊重自己，不容许别人歧视或侮辱的心理。
English MeaningThe psychology of respecting oneself and not allowing others to discriminate or insult.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 自 + 尊 + 心 = 自尊心
自 (zì) = self  |  尊 (zūn) = to respect; honor  |  心 (xīn) = heart; mind
→ A heart that respects itself.
📝 Example 不要伤害孩子的自尊心。 Don't hurt a child's self-esteem.
感性 gǎnxìng emotional; sensitive; perceptual
中文释义凭感觉、情感来判断和行动的方式。
English MeaningA way of judging and acting based on feelings and emotions.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 感 + 性 = 感性
感 (gǎn) = to feel; emotion  |  性 (xìng) = nature; quality; disposition
→ An emotional nature.
📝 Example 她是个很感性的人，看电影经常哭。 She is a very emotional person and often cries during movies.
理性 lǐxìng rational; reason
中文释义用逻辑和思考来判断和行动的方式。
English MeaningA way of judging and acting using logic and reasoning.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 理 + 性 = 理性
理 (lǐ) = reason; logic; principle  |  性 (xìng) = nature; disposition
→ A rational nature.
📝 Example 面对问题，我们需要理性思考。 When facing problems, we need to think rationally.
意志力 yìzhì lì willpower
中文释义控制自己的行为、情绪和冲动的能力。
English MeaningThe ability to control one's behavior, emotions, and impulses.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 意志 + 力 = 意志力
意志 (yìzhì) = will; determination  |  力 (lì) = power; strength
→ The power of will.
📝 Example 戒烟需要很强的意志力。 Quitting smoking requires strong willpower.
冷静 lěngjìng calm; cool-headed
中文释义情绪稳定，不慌张、不激动的状态。
English MeaningA state of emotional stability; not panicked or agitated.
🔍 Word Deconstruction 冷 + 静 = 冷静
冷 (lěng) = cold; cool  |  静 (jìng) = quiet; still; calm
→ Cool and calm.
📝 Example 遇到紧急情况要保持冷静。 Stay calm in an emergency.
